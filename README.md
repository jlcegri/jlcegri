## 👋 About me

I'm a **Computer Engineering graduate (Software Engineering)** from the University of Seville, now pursuing a **Master's in Computer Security Engineering and Artificial Intelligence** at Universitat Rovira i Virgili.

I enjoy building full-stack products end to end — from architecture and testing to deployment — and I'm increasingly drawn to the intersection of **security**, **decentralized systems** and **machine learning**.

- 🔭 Currently focused on **Cybersecurity** and **Artificial Intelligence**
- 🌱 Learning **cloud & container orchestration** (Docker, Kubernetes)
- ⛓️ Experimenting with **Web3**: Solidity, Hardhat, The Graph, IPFS
- 🌍 Erasmus+ at the **University of Coimbra** (Portugal) — I work comfortably in English (C1) and Portuguese (B2)
- 💬 Ask me about: full-stack development, smart contracts, ML pipelines, clean code
